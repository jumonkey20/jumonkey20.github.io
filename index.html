Pseudomonas oprD Gene & Resistance Profiling

Bioinformatics / Python

An automated bioinformatics pipeline designed to analyze genetic divergence and antimicrobial resistance markers in Pseudomonas aeruginosa strains.

Biological Context:

Targets the oprD gene encoding the outer-membrane porin channel OprD, a major entry port for carbapenem antibiotics. Mutations or identity drops lead to clinical multidrug resistance.

Pipeline Methodology:
  • Data Parsing: Utilizes Biopython's SeqIO to ingest multi-FASTA files containing reference strain PAO1 (Accession: NP_249649.1) and variant clinical isolates.
  • Global Alignment: Implements the Needleman-Wunsch algorithm via Biopython's PairwiseAligner() with custom gap scores (open: -10, extend: -0.5).
  • Quantification & Export: Programmatically calculates match counts, total lengths, and percentage identities, structuring results into a Pandas DataFrame exported as alignment_results.csv.

p53 & PRIMA-1 Molecular Docking Pipeline

Structural Biology

A virtual screening and structural bioinformatics workflow investigating ligand-protein binding interactions to evaluate pharmacological restoration of mutant p53 proteins.

Biological Context:

Mutations in the tumor suppressor protein p53 are prevalent in human cancers. PRIMA-1 acts as a small molecule capable of reactivating mutant p53 conformations.

Pipeline Methodology:
  • Structure Preparation: Imported target macromolecule PDB structures (such as 1TUP) and prepared ligand coordinate files into PDBQT format.
  • Grid Box Optimization: Defined active binding site coordinates and bounding boxes to bound conformational search spaces.
  • Execution & Scoring: Executed AutoDock Vina calculations via PyRx to compute binding affinities (kcal/mol) and rank docking conformations.
